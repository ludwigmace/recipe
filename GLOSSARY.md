# Recipe Library

This context helps a household collect, find, and cook from recipes together.

## Language

**Household**:
A group of people who share one recipe library.

**Household member**:
A person invited into a household. Members can invite others and manage recipes in the shared library.

**Recipe library**:
A household's collection of saved recipes.

**Recipe**:
Cooking instructions organized by a title, ingredients, and method.

**Saved recipe**:
A recipe kept as a stable copy in the household's library, with its source webpage retained separately. Members can correct the shared copy.
_Avoid_: Bookmark

**Recipe source**:
The original webpage from which a recipe was captured.

**Archived recipe**:
A saved recipe removed from normal search but retained so a household member can restore it.
